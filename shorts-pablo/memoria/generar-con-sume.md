For AI images and video clips (scenes with Pablo, B-roll recreations), use the **Sume** MCP (server url https://mcp.sume.com/mcp; tools generate_image / generate_video / media_upload / jobs_wait, quote with `dry_run: true`, prices come in USD). ElevenLabs is only for music (Music v2.5) as agreed in [[flujo-shorts-pablo]].

**Why:** on 2026-11-13 I started the Kindle Short's AI scene in an ElevenLabs flow and he corrected: "debes usar Sume, ese es el MCP que tenemos para generar las imágenes".

**How to apply:** upload his reference photos (marca-pablo-video/assets/pablo/) with Sume media_upload, dry_run to quote in USD, ask before the paid run, label the result on screen as AI.
